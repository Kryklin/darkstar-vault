<h1 align="center">Contributor Covenant Code of Conduct</h1>

## <img src="assets/icons/standards.svg" width="20" height="20" align="absmiddle" alt="Pledge" /> Our Pledge

We as members, contributors, and maintainers pledge to make participation in the Darkstar Vault community a harassment-free and empowering experience for everyone, regardless of age, body size, visible or invisible disability, ethnicity, sex, gender identity and expression, level of experience, education, socio-economic status, nationality, personal appearance, race, caste, religion, or sexual identity and orientation.

We pledge to act and interact in ways that contribute to an open, welcoming, diverse, inclusive, and healthy community.

---

## <img src="assets/icons/check.svg" width="20" height="20" align="absmiddle" alt="Standards" /> Our Standards

Examples of behavior that contributes to a positive environment for our community include:

- Demonstrating empathy and kindness toward other people.
- Being respectful of differing opinions, viewpoints, and experiences.
- Giving and gracefully accepting constructive feedback.
- Accepting responsibility, apologizing to those affected by our mistakes, and learning from the experience.
- Focusing on what is best not just for us as individuals, but for the overall community and project security.

Examples of unacceptable behavior include:

- The use of sexualized language or imagery, and sexual attention or advances of any kind.
- Trolling, insulting or derogatory comments, and personal or political attacks.
- Public or private harassment.
- Publishing others' private information (doxxing), such as physical or email addresses, without explicit permission.
- Other conduct which could reasonably be considered inappropriate in a professional setting.

---

## <img src="assets/icons/shield.svg" width="20" height="20" align="absmiddle" alt="Enforcement" /> Enforcement Responsibilities

Community maintainers are responsible for clarifying and enforcing our standards of acceptable behavior and will take appropriate and fair corrective action in response to any behavior that they deem inappropriate, threatening, offensive, or harmful.

Community maintainers have the right and responsibility to remove, edit, or reject comments, commits, code, wiki edits, issues, and other contributions that are not aligned to this Code of Conduct, and will communicate reasons for moderation decisions when appropriate.

---

## <img src="assets/icons/lock.svg" width="20" height="20" align="absmiddle" alt="Scope" /> Scope & Reporting

This Code of Conduct applies within all project spaces (GitHub repositories, issue trackers, discussions) and also applies when an individual is officially representing the project in public spaces.

Instances of abusive, harassing, or otherwise unacceptable behavior may be reported to the community maintainers at:  
**<kalemslight@gmail.com>**  
*(Alternative contact: `<mortalpain1@gmail.com>`)*

All reports will be reviewed and investigated promptly and fairly. All maintainers are obligated to respect the privacy and security of the reporter of any incident.
